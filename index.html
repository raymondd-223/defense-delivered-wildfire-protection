Why homes burn

It's not the flames. It's the embers.

More than 90% of homes lost in wildfires are ignited by flying embers, not direct flames.

Wind-driven embers travel miles ahead of the fire front, landing in gutters, vents, decks and landscaping, and igniting fires long before you ever see a flame. Even homes located two to three miles from the forest edge can be reduced to ash in minutes. Fire crews may not be able to reach you in time, and are sometimes forced to retreat when overrun by fire and ember storms.

Defense Delivered installs automated exterior suppression systems and home-hardening solutions that target exactly where embers land: rooftops, vents, gutters, decks and the surrounding 30-foot defensible zone. Our systems activate when you are away, soaking vulnerable areas in advance so your home can survive when fire crews cannot intervene.

Three steps, in order. Each one builds on the last, and each one is documented so your insurance carrier can see exactly what was done.

2

Harden It

Protect the first five feet

The area within five feet of your home is the most critical. We clear flammable vegetation, replace mulch with gravel or stone, and install fine mesh to block embers from entering vents, eaves and gutters. We make your home fire resistant.

Clear the ignition zone

Flammable vegetation and mulch within five feet of the structure are removed or replaced with non-combustible gravel and stone.

Seal the entry points

Fine ember-resistant mesh on vents, eaves and gutters keeps embers from getting inside the attic and crawl spaces.

Work outward in zones

Defensible space recommendations extend to the 30-foot zone and beyond, following Firewise USA and IBHS guidance.

Automated wildfire suppression system

After home hardening, our Real Intelligence-powered automated exterior sprinkler suppression system is your last line of defense. With data fusion from thermal cameras, satellite wildfire alerts and temperature sensors, the system activates automatically as fire danger approaches your property, using a graduated response: first pre-wet exterior surfaces, then soak them, and finally deploy US Forest Service-approved Class A foam if needed.

Defense Delivered has the most advanced automated external wildfire suppression system on the market, at the lowest cost of any comparable system available today.

  • Class A firefighting foam, reported to be up to five times more effective than water alone
  • Battery backup on every system, with optional cellular communication

Insurance, equity and financing

Your home can survive a wildfire if you prepare in advance

Even with good insurance coverage, a policy is limited to the replacement value to rebuild. Mitigation protects the equity and the irreplaceable possessions a policy cannot.

Insurance coverage and discounts

Colorado insurance companies have begun denying coverage to homes in the Wildland-Urban Interface zone, or dramatically increasing fire insurance rates. Colorado House Bill 25-1182, passed in May 2025, addresses this exact topic. Beginning in July 2026, Colorado insurers must consider all wildfire mitigation efforts in their rate and coverage decisions and provide discounts for property-specific and community mitigation.

Your Summit Fire/EMS wildfire assessment report, documenting your remediation work with before-and-after photographs, can be submitted to your insurer for rate reductions and discounts. This is where mitigation pays for itself over a period of years.

Protect your equity

If your home is valued at $2 million and your policy covers $1 million, the equity balance disappears until you have rebuilt, a process that can easily take two years.

Your return on investment is insuring or protecting that equity value, and your irreplaceable possessions, with active home fire protection.

Colorado House Bill 24-1091 also voids HOA covenants and deed restrictions that prohibit fire-resistant building materials, so associations cannot stand in the way of hardening your home.

Frequently asked questions

Protection, insurance, pricing, installation and technology

Most homes lost in wildfires are ignited by embers landing on roofs, decks and landscaping before flames ever reach the structure. Our system detects danger early and automatically creates a protective moisture and humidity barrier around your home while you evacuate safely.

Homes

How does the system protect my home?

Our external wildfire suppression system pre-wets your roof, decks, siding and landscaping to prevent embers and radiant heat from igniting your home. It builds a defensive moisture barrier before the fire arrives, giving firefighters, insurance carriers and you more time and confidence.

Will I run out of water?

Most homes have access to more water than they realize. Our system uses far less water than the competition and is easily supplied by a municipal supply. Underground or above-ground storage tanks are used on properties without municipal water or on large homes. Pools, ponds and cisterns may be used, and pumps can maintain pressure even if the utility shuts down. We size your system to protect your home throughout the wildfire threat window.

Will this work if I evacuate or lose power?

Yes. All systems include battery backup. Optional cellular communication keeps the system online even if the internet or power fails.

How long will it run during a fire?

Run time is variable and adjustable based on wind, humidity, water supply and fire proximity. A wildfire can be two miles away and generating dangerous embers, so the system is designed to raise humidity and saturate the home to prevent ignition, sometimes two days or more before the fire approaches.

Will my HOA approve this?

Almost always. Our components are discreet and low-profile, and we provide HOA submission packets with clear renderings and safety documentation. Colorado House Bill 24-1091 also voids HOA covenants that prohibit fire-resistant materials.

Do you only serve Colorado?

We serve Colorado and the Western United States, and we travel nationwide for larger estates, rural properties, religious and retreat centers and commercial infrastructure.

Insurance

Will this help with my insurance?

Possibly. Insurance companies in Colorado and across the West may require wildfire mitigation for policy renewals. Our system provides documented wildfire risk suppression that may qualify you for discounts, reduced deductibles and a better chance of maintaining coverage. We help with hardening and mitigation and gather the documentation your carrier needs.

What documentation do insurers want to see?

Recommended criteria for mitigation credits include:

  • Installation documentation: professional installation certificate, as-built diagrams and component specs
  • Annual inspection: a third-party CWMS report verifying coverage, operation, and freeze and backflow protection
  • Operational readiness: test activation logs and maintenance records demonstrating over 90% readiness
  • System design: zoning, short-burst programming, wall and eave-directed nozzles, verified backup water and power
  • Home hardening: proof of defensible space and ember-resistant retrofits
Is the assessment really free?

Yes. There is no cost for the inspection or the report. It is provided through your local fire department in partnership with Defense Delivered. Most reports are completed within 7 to 10 days of the onsite visit, and the report is insurance-ready.

Do I have to use Defense Delivered for the mitigation work?

No. The assessment is completely independent and you can choose any contractor. Our team is available to help complete the recommended improvements if you would like.

Condos, HOAs and commercial

Can your system protect multi-unit buildings?

Yes. We design wildfire sprinkler protection for condominiums, townhomes and shared-roof buildings, including rooftops, eaves, common decks and landscaping. Most multi-unit projects are completed in two to four weeks with no occupant disruption.

Can we apply for grants?

Yes. Funding sources may include FEMA BRIC and HMGP grants, state wildfire resilience programs, DOE and utility wildfire-risk reduction incentives, and certain critical-infrastructure cost-share programs. We guide applications and compliance and provide the engineering and hazard-mitigation documentation required.

Who pays for installation?

Associations typically use a combination of reserves, special assessments, grants and insurance incentives. Defense Delivered offers financing for mitigation and wildfire suppression systems up to $500,000, and we help boards communicate clearly with residents.

Will this affect the building's appearance?

We believe our installations should be an extension of the existing property, and we build them to match the unique characteristics of any building. Harmonious, functional design remains our top priority.

Can we integrate with existing security systems?

Yes. Our controllers integrate with fire panels, site safety dashboards and network monitoring centers. Real-time alerts by SMS, email and app support command-center decision-making.

Do you provide service and maintenance?

Yes. We offer annual inspection plans, winterization and emergency support to ensure your system is ready year-round.
